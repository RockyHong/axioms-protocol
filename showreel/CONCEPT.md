# AXIOMS: concept version v2 (64s, text only, voiceover-ready)

**Rule 1: one focal point at a time.** The camera follows the attention dot. Every caption appears where the eye already is, and text and action take turns rather than competing.
**Rule 2: blue means attention.** Attention is a donut split into 12 slices, and its fixed capacity is what "finite" means. Every time a slice leaves or comes back, the screen shows why.
Palette: paper `#F7F7F5` · ink `#111113` · grey `#8E8E93` · blue `#0A5CFF`.
