# Axioms

A closed, substrate-neutral canon of finite-actor agency — and two skills to hold your work against it.

- [`AXIOMS.md`](AXIOMS.md) — the canon: apex, fuel, scope, and the seven axioms.
- `/axioms:audit <target>` — pin the target and its outcome-anchor with you, then dispatch a fresh-context agent that audits a topic, design, or decision against the canon. Surfaces grounded concerns, possible antipatterns, and insights; proposes options. You judge.
- `/axioms:load <topic>` — load the canon and think a topic through in open conversation grounded in it. No procedure, no findings report — the canon frames, you steer.
