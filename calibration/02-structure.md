## Seam resolutions (my leanings — flag if wrong)

1. **Boundary is the partition primitive** — it serves *Divide* (partition attention = "SoC = attention boundary distribution") **and** *Preserve* (partition truth-ownership = SSOT's "clear pillars"). → Home it in Divide; cross-reference from Preserve. One axiom, not two. (Validates your "Boundary = ownership + routing": routing is half the axiom — see below.)
2. **Economy is cross-cutting, not a third axis** — cost hits both now-spend (wasted attention) and stored-state (orphan artifacts). → Keep it policing both, not a peer axis.
3. **Aim sits atop Axis A, not a separate pre-layer** — pointing attention at an outcome is itself a cognitive act done in the moment. → Keeps your clean two-axis top level (SPEND vs KEEP).

**Routing is rich** — your "Boundary = ownership + routing" absorbs three seed elements under its routing half:
- forward-route at an edge ("if not me, who") = *positive ≥ negative (rejection = wall, routing = path)*
- route-up when blocked = *escalate*
- route-out to capability = the bridge to *Leverage/Extend*
